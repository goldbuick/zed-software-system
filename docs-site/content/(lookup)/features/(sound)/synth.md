## Write a sound

1. [Synth voices](/synth/voices) — pick `#synth` families, knobs, and pasteable recipes
2. [Effects](/synth/effects) — sends, bus suffixes (`#echo` vs `#echo3`), starter presets
3. [Notation](/synth/playnotation) — `#play` characters (digits are drums)
4. [Scales](/synth/scales) — ready-made scale lines
