## books and pages

$whitelist books in memory; open or create

$whiteswitch the opened (live) book by id or name

$whitecreate an empty book without opening it
#pages
$whitelist pages in all loaded books
#boards
$whitelist boards in all loaded books
#search
$whitesearch pages and boards across all loaded books
@[pagetype:]page name
$whitecreate and edit a new codepage in the opened book
#trash
$whitelist books and pages you can delete (page trash is the opened book)
