Commands and play notation stay in the front-end. DaisySP WASM renders the audio. Docs split into **Play** (what you type) and **Internals** (how audio is wired).

## Start here if you are writing music

1. [Synth voices](/synth/voices) — pick a voice, learn the knobs, paste an A/B recipe
2. [Effects](/synth/effects) — echo, reverb, distort, vibrato, and the other sends
3. [Notation](/synth/playnotation) — how `#play` characters become notes (digits are drums)
4. [Scales](/synth/scales) — copy-paste `#play` lines by key and mode

In-game helper scroll opens voices, effects, and scales directly.

## Play

What you type: a voice, an effect send, a scale, or the characters in a `#play` line.

- [Synth](/synth/voices) -- `#synth` voices and config keys
- [Effects](/synth/effects) -- echo, reverb, and the other voice FX
- [Notation](/synth/playnotation) -- how play text becomes notes
- [Scales](/synth/scales) -- `#play` lines for each scale and key
